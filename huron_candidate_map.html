Hosting Capacity (kW)

Hosting Capacity represents the electricity capacity provided by DTE at the location of each candidate site. This value indicates the available electrical capacity that could support DC fast charging infrastructure at that location.

The hosting capacity value for each candidate site is shown in the site popup when you click on a candidate marker.
